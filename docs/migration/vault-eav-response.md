## Remaining source and field-contract work

- V01 external originals: exact source selection, source revisions, and verified
  correspondence to the ongoing Conversation. Equal text/URLs are insufficient.
- Typed source associations and structural provenance links, including Observation
  `place` and ContextInput `suppliedContext`, with roles and exact historical
  endpoints. These remain distinct from Topic membership and Wiki Review.
- Exact saved Query/chronology fixtures, retained result evidence and ordering.
  Initial individual-record embeds do not establish those source shapes.
- Calendar time-value coverage for source minute/second precision, original
  expression/offset/timezone, explicit null and unknown timing. Admission time is
  not occurrence, completion or capture time.
- Root integration and full implementation CI are complete. Merge interaction
  and direct route adapters are beyond the current redirect basis. Verify the
  final merged main commit and its deployment separately.
- Exhaustive independent reopening and source-backed verification of the new
  migration. No conversion is certified by the application's owner fixtures alone.
