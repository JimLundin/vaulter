# Source translation and knowledge reconstruction

The application uses one native EAV journal under [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-eav-only-vault.md).
Source-specific inventories, historical format interpretation, identity mapping, personal examples and
publication procedures belong to the private Vault under [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-vault-owned-translation.md).
This note describes review principles rather than an application migration command.

Retain original prose, independently captured sources, honest occurrence precision and attribution.
Format validation does not certify factual support. Reconstruct identities, qualifiers, effective time
and evidence as separately reviewed work. Topic organization is an editorial mapping distinct from a
factual relationship. Narrower material can share a broad Topic without merging its identity or claims.

A verified publication targets an implemented, pinned Vaulter revision and its owner schemas. Preserve
source evidence and chronology, validate historical selections and read back accepted target records.
A current-state export cannot claim to reproduce unavailable source history. Capture the baseline,
source revision and tooling provenance; revalidate when those inputs change. Accepted EAV history is
immutable and corrections append new transactions.

Review selected assertions and summaries independently. A supported clause does not support the rest
of its paragraph; page provenance does not support any statement. Preserve unresolved identities,
ambiguous time, unknown outcomes and missing references rather than manufacturing certainty.
Source capture and prose review can precede qualified structured relationships, interpreted events,
commitments or a cross-domain review queue. Publish each new domain only through its implemented owner.

Verify uncertain acknowledgement and reopen against the same finalized publication without duplicate
records or replayed effects. Measure promoted Topics, retrieved/rendered records and edges separately.
Report assertion coverage, disagreements, unresolved identities and lifecycle outcomes independently.
See the [data model](../data-model.md) for current contracts and remaining domain capabilities.
